## What is EcoItems?

EcoItems is a Minecraft plugin that adds fully customizable weapons, tools, armor, foods, and items to your server, each defined in its own config file. You decide the display, the components, the recipe, and the behaviour, so an item can be anything from a simple crafting ingredient to a piece of god gear with custom effects.

## What sets EcoItems apart?

- **Driven by the eco effects system:** attach the same effects, triggers, and conditions used across every eco plugin, no code required.
- **Full item component support:** set any vanilla item component (food, tools, attributes, cooldowns, and everything else) with the same format as vanilla commands.
- **Custom textures (paid version):** give items their own textures and models, delivered to players automatically with a generated resource pack.
- **Custom blocks, furniture, and crops:** placeable [blocks](how-to-make-a-block) (with stripping and stacking), [furniture](how-to-make-furniture) with seats, doors, storage, connecting rows, and even driveable vehicles, and staged [crops](how-to-make-a-crop).
- **Loot injection:** drop custom items from vanilla [blocks, mobs, and fishing](loot-injection).
- **Custom paintings (paid version):** ship fully custom [paintings](how-to-make-a-painting) with their own artwork and size.
- **Craftable items:** give any item a shaped or shapeless recipe, with an optional crafting permission. Item [templates](how-to-make-an-item#templates) keep big item sets DRY.
- **Rarity system:** categorize items by rarity with lore tags and weights.
